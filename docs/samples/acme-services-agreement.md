# Acme Cloud Services Agreement (Sample)

## 1. Parties and term
This Cloud Services Agreement is made on 1 March 2026 between Acme Cloud Ltd ("Provider") and Northwind Retail Inc. ("Customer"). The initial term is 24 months, starting on 1 April 2026. The agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the end of the current term.

## 2. Services and service levels
Provider will host the Customer's e-commerce platform, data warehouse and backups. Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime for production systems, measured excluding scheduled maintenance. Scheduled maintenance happens on Sundays between 01:00 and 04:00 UTC and is announced at least 5 business days in advance. Priority 1 incidents (full outage) receive a response within 30 minutes and a status update every hour until resolved. Priority 2 incidents receive a response within 4 hours.

## 3. Fees and payment
The Customer pays a fixed platform fee of USD 18,500 per month plus usage charges for storage above 40 TB at USD 21 per additional terabyte. Invoices are issued monthly in advance and are due within 30 days. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month. Fees may increase by no more than 5% at each renewal, with 90 days' written notice.

## 4. Service credits and refunds
If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the Customer receives a service credit of 10% of the monthly platform fee. Below 99.0%, the credit rises to 25%. Credits are the Customer's sole remedy for availability failures and are capped at 50% of the monthly fee. During the first 30 days after the start date, the Customer may cancel for any reason and receive a full refund of fees already paid, minus usage charges.

## 5. Data protection and security
Provider stores all Customer data in the EU (Frankfurt and Dublin regions) and encrypts it at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Provider must notify the Customer of any personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware of it. Backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 35 days. Provider completes an independent SOC 2 Type II audit every year and shares the report on request.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days of written notice. The Customer may terminate for convenience after the first 12 months by paying a termination fee equal to 3 months of platform fees. On termination, Provider keeps Customer data available for export for 45 days and then deletes it permanently within a further 30 days.

## 7. Liability
Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. This cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality, data protection obligations, or gross negligence.

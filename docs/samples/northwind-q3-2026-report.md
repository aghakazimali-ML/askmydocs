# Northwind Retail: Q3 2026 Business Report (Sample)

## 1. Executive summary
Northwind Retail closed Q3 2026 with revenue of USD 42.6 million, up 14% year on year and 6% above plan. Online sales grew 31% and now make up 38% of total revenue. Operating margin improved from 7.9% to 9.4%, mainly because of lower shipping costs and fewer returns. The migration to Acme Cloud finished on schedule on 15 July.

## 2. Revenue by channel
Stores generated USD 26.4 million (down 2% year on year) while e-commerce generated USD 16.2 million (up 31%). The mobile app passed 1.2 million monthly active users, and app customers spend on average 23% more per order than website customers. Average order value online rose from USD 64 to USD 71.

## 3. Customers and marketing
The loyalty program reached 480,000 members, a gain of 62,000 in the quarter. Repeat purchase rate improved to 41%. Customer acquisition cost fell from USD 38 to USD 31 after paid social spend moved to email and SMS campaigns. Net Promoter Score rose from 34 to 42.

## 4. Operations
Delivery time for online orders dropped from 3.8 days to 2.6 days after opening the new Leeds fulfilment centre in August. Return rate fell from 11.5% to 8.9%, helped by better size guides on product pages. Inventory turnover improved to 6.1 times per year.

## 5. Risks
Two main risks were identified for Q4. First, supplier delays in South-East Asia could affect up to 18% of holiday inventory; the team is placing early orders and adding a second supplier for top-selling lines. Second, cloud costs rose 12% in Q3 because of data warehouse growth above the 40 TB contract threshold; a storage clean-up project aims to save USD 9,000 per month. Smaller risks include rising card fees and a shortage of seasonal warehouse staff.

## 6. Outlook and priorities for Q4
Management expects Q4 revenue between USD 55 and 58 million, driven by the holiday season. Priorities are: launch same-day delivery in London by 15 November, grow loyalty membership to 550,000, keep the return rate below 9%, and complete the storage clean-up by 31 December. Capital spending for Q4 is budgeted at USD 2.3 million, mostly for warehouse automation.
